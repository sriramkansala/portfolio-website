We are nerds.

I design for clarity, not engagement. For the moment a person understands exactly what to do next without being asked. For software that trusts the people who use it.

I believe good design is invisible. The best interface is the one nobody notices — because it just works, every time, the way they expected. That's the bar I set for everything I ship.

I care about the details that don't show up in a sprint review: the wording of an error message, the weight of a button under a thumb, the silence of a loading state that doesn't panic. Done well, these add up to something that simply feels right.

I'm small on purpose — no growth targets, no trend chasing. One designer who has thought deeply about every part of the work.
